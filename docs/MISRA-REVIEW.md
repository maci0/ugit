## Scanner suppressions

Five narrowly explained suppressions remain, with no rule waived:

- Two `deprecated_api` findings confuse current `std.fs.path.isAbsolute` and
  `std.fs.path.basename` with deprecated `std.Io.Dir.path`.
- Two `use_after_close` findings are assignments replacing closed directory
  handles with newly opened child handles.
- One `try_before_release` finding misses ownership transferred into an
  already-reserved tree queue; the queue's defer releases that path.

The actual findings also led to removing an unreferenced test module and fixing
initialization/ownership patterns before the final scan. No checker source or
rule was changed to obtain a clean result.
